Case study 01 · Sep to Oct 2026

VITCO Impex: Meta click-to-message ads

Meta Ads Messages objective Lead generation Creative direction

The goal

Turn people scrolling past an ad into real conversations: buyers asking about VITCO's khoya and cream machines, and candidates enquiring about a job opening, all through a single tap on the ad.

What I did

I set up five Meta campaigns on the Messages objective, each with its own creative and its own small daily budget of about ₹150 to ₹193. Running every product and offer as a separate campaign kept the results easy to compare, so I could see exactly which message people responded to.

What the data showed

The two product-led ads, Khoya Machine and Cream Machine, earned conversations at roughly ₹10 to ₹11 each. The hiring ad and the broader offers cost three to five times more per conversation. That tells me where to put the next rupee: product-specific creatives first, and sharper targeting for the wider offers.
